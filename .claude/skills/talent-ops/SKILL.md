# Talent Ops

Workflow for `businesses/acting-modeling/`. Read `BUSINESS.md` first — starting position, lane
(modeling-led), stats, and the hard constraints.

## Rule zero: vet before anything reaches Austin

Any agency, casting notice, "scout," or opportunity gets run through this before it is passed
through. Auto-fail on any of these:

- Upfront fee of any kind (signing, registration, website placement, portfolio, admin)
- Required in-house photographer or paid "training" as a condition
- Guaranteed bookings or guaranteed income
- Pressure to decide immediately
- DM/WhatsApp/Telegram-only contact with no verifiable business
- A domain that is *nearly* a real agency's, or a free-mail address claiming to be one
- Requests for ID, bank details, a verification payment, or sensitive photos
- Wants measurements and personal photos before identifying themselves

Survivors get verified: real address, years in operation, staff who appear elsewhere in the
industry, verifiable past credits, and — for productions — a cross-check against the
[NC Film Office current productions list](https://www.filmnc.com/current-productions).

Legitimate agencies earn **commission on booked work** (commonly ~10–20%). Money flowing from
Austin to an agency before he has earned anything is the scam signature.

## Asset build order

Do not skip ahead; each step makes the next one cheaper and better.

1. **Stats sheet** — height, weight, chest/waist/inseam, shoe, suit, shirt/neck, hair, eyes, age
   range, union status, transport, availability. Free, and blocks everything downstream.
2. **Digitals** — plain wall, natural light, no makeup, fitted clothes, front/side/back, one
   smiling and one neutral. Free. Agencies weight these heavily for new faces.
3. **Tearsheets collected** — the Flair Magazine editorial, gathered into `portfolio/`.
4. **Professional headshots** — the one item worth real money. Commercial + theatrical looks.
5. **Comp card** — 5.5"×8.5", one main image, 3–4 supporting, stats on the back.
6. **Resume** — one page, honest, industry-standard order.
7. **Casting profiles** — free platforms first; one paid subscription only after headshots exist.
8. **Reel** — downstream. Requires footage from real bookings; not a blocker for anything above.

## Submission package format

One package per target, drafted into `submissions/`:

- Short professional email — who he is, market, union status, what's attached, nothing more
- Headshot + digitals + comp card + resume, named clearly (`Malouin_Austin_Headshot.jpg`)
- Follow the target's stated submission format **exactly** — agencies reject on format before
  they look at a single photo
- Log it: `submissions/YYYY-MM-DD-<target>.md` with what was sent, to whom, and the outcome once
  known. This log is how follow-up timing gets decided later.

## Weekly casting sweep

Written to `casting/` as a dated file, per the format in `casting-scout-agent`. Lead with the two
or three worth acting on this week; everything else below. State pay plainly, including "unpaid."
An honest empty week is a valid result.

## Handoff

Nothing here goes out on its own. Agency emails, applications, replies, captions, and post plans
are drafted into `inbox/` and Austin sends or publishes them himself — see the draft-and-recommend
model in [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md). This matters more in this venture than any other, because
these are real people in a small local industry and Austin's name is permanently attached.
