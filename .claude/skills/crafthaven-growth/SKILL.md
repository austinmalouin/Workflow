# CraftHaven Growth

Workflow for `businesses/crafthaven/`, a real joinable Minecraft Java Edition server. Read
`BUSINESS.md` first for current state — Houses, hosting tier, launch timing.

## Content calendar

Maintain `content-calendar/` with in-server events that give real players a reason to log in and
give the `minecraft-channel` footage to cut episodes from: House elections, war/siege windows,
build contests, and Throne-holding contests — timed against the channel's upload schedule so
server events and video releases reinforce each other.

## Lore/House framework

Keep the server's House roster, sigils, and the Throne mechanic's in-game naming current in
`marketing/` — original names only (no HBO-owned GoT terms; see the IP note in `BUSINESS.md`).

## Cross-promotion

Research (WebSearch) where the existing GoT/medieval-Minecraft audience already congregates —
relevant subreddits, faction-server Discords, "best medieval servers" round-ups — and note
realistic, non-spammy ways to make CraftHaven visible there. Most communities have explicit
self-promotion rules; find and respect them rather than assuming blanket posting is fine.

## Drafting posts

Write announcement/event posts into `marketing/` or directly as calendar entries — Discord-native
formatting/tone (Discord is the support/community layer here, not the product) and ready to paste
with zero editing.

## Handoff

Finished posts go to `inbox/` for Austin to post manually — there's no hosting or bot/server
connector yet, so nothing here goes live on its own.
