# Submissions

One file per submission actually sent: `YYYY-MM-DD-<target>.md`.

Record: who it went to, exactly which assets were attached, the date, any stated response window,
and the outcome once known (no response / rejected / callback / booked).

Why this log matters more than it looks: follow-up timing is most of the game early on. Knowing
you submitted to an agency six weeks ago and never heard back is what tells you it's appropriate
to follow up once — and knowing you already followed up is what stops you from becoming the person
who emails four times.

It's also the only honest measure of activity in the first 90 days. Bookings are mostly outside
Austin's control; submissions sent are entirely inside it. That's the number to watch.

**Nothing in this folder is sent by an agent.** Drafts live in `inbox/` until Austin sends them
himself; this folder records what he sent.
