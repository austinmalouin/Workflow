# Devlog #1 — "Building a Kingdom From Nothing"

Series: Houses of the Throne (companion devlog, not a numbered episode) | Format: behind-the-scenes
devlog — screen capture (hosting panel, planning docs, whiteboard/concept sketches) + narration, no
gameplay or player footage required | Est. runtime: 5-7 minutes | Voice: voiceover (face-optional) |
**Drafted 2026-08-17**

## Why this video, why now

Grounded in research pulled this session (2026-08-17), not assumed:

1. **Devlog/build-in-public content is a validated, current trend.** 2026 Minecraft YouTube research
   (Filmora, Packapop, FluxNote roundups) confirms server/SMP content and "compelling Minecraft lore
   or storytelling" videos are performing well, and that series-based content outperforms one-offs.
   A devlog series is a standard, proven on-ramp for server-building channels — it turns planning
   work that already happened into real footage without needing players or a live server.
2. **It's the one piece of unblocked content the channel currently has.** Episode -1 (the House
   teaser, `teaser-five-houses.md`) is blocked on Austin confirming House names; Episodes 0-4 need
   real server/player footage that doesn't exist yet. A devlog needs neither — it documents
   decisions already made and logged in `../../crafthaven/BUSINESS.md` (hosting tier, monetization
   rules, naming caution), which can be screen-recorded and narrated today.
3. **It builds audience trust before launch.** Kingdom/faction-server channels that show real
   planning (hosting cost, EULA-compliant monetization rules stated up front) read as more credible
   than a cold launch video with no track record — this also pre-empts the most common criticism of
   monetized Minecraft servers (pay-to-win backlash) by stating the rules publicly before a single
   item is sold.

**Sits in the schedule outside the numbered arc** — see the new row in
`content-calendar/launch-arc-schedule.md`. Can release before or after Episode -1; doesn't compete
with it since it's a different content type (process, not lore-reveal).

## Beat 1 — The gap (0:15-1:30)

Screen: quick side-by-side stills/text cards, not full gameplay — WesterosCraft screenshots (credited),
an Empires SMP clip reference (described, not used without rights), a generic kingdom-server listing.

**VO talking points:**
- Kingdom/faction Minecraft servers are a proven, populated genre — this isn't a new mechanic.
- The GoT-adjacent audience is real and patient (cite WesterosCraft's 12+ year run) but underserved
  as an actual *game* — it's a museum you walk through, not a world you fight over.
- The gap: nobody's combined GoT-adjacent political theming + a real public server anyone can join +
  a serialized YouTube series turning real server events into episodes. That's the bet.

## Beat 2 — The naming problem (1:30-2:30)

Screen: a whiteboard/notes-app shot of crossed-out names ("Iron Throne", "House Stark") next to the
final picks.

**VO talking points:**
- "Game of Thrones" is an actively defended HBO trademark — can't use the real names on something
  built to make money.
- So: original names built to evoke the feeling without borrowing the IP. Server name: CraftHaven.
  The Throne mechanic: the Broken Seat — contested, nobody holds it for good.
- Frame as a creative constraint, not just a legal footnote — "reskinning it forced better names than
  just reusing the real ones would have."

## Beat 3 — The hosting decision (2:30-3:45)

Screen: real Apex Hosting pricing table (from `crafthaven/BUSINESS.md`), cursor circling the 4 GB
Basic tier.

**VO talking points:**
- Real numbers, not vague: Apex Hosting, Basic 4 GB tier, $11.24 first month / $14.99 recurring.
- Why the cheap tier and not headroom for "hoped-for growth": a brand-new server has zero players at
  launch — no reason to pay for capacity nobody's using yet. Upgrade when Houses are actually filling
  up, not before.
- This is a small, real business decision, stated on camera — not just gameplay content.

## Beat 4 — The monetization promise (3:45-5:15)

Screen: a simple text-card list — "WILL sell" / "WILL NEVER sell" — built from the EULA-compliant
plan already locked in `crafthaven/BUSINESS.md`.

**VO talking points:**
- State the rules before anything is built, so nobody discovers them later and feels burned: cosmetic
  ranks, cosmetic crate keys, land-claim *convenience* (not advantage) — never a kit or item that
  gives a paying player a combat or war edge a free player can't ever match through play.
- This is the single most common way monetized servers get community backlash — naming it up front is
  a trust move, not just a compliance one.
