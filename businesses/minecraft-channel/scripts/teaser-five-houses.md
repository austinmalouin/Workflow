# Episode -1 (Teaser) — "Five Houses, One Empty Throne"

Series: Houses of the Throne | Format: short-form lore-reveal teaser (cinematic stills/concept art
+ narration, not full gameplay) | Est. runtime: 90 seconds - 2:30 (built to double as a Short and a
short-form YouTube upload) | Voice: voiceover (face-optional) | **Drafted 2026-08-10**

## Why this video, why now

Grounded in two pieces of research pulled this session (2026-08-10), not assumed:

1. **Pre-launch content gap.** `content-calendar/launch-arc-schedule.md` is honest that Episode 0
   needs real spawn/build footage and Episodes 1-4 need real player activity that can't exist until
   the server is live — but the channel currently has zero public content and the server has no
   launch date yet. A short, cheap-to-produce teaser that needs only concept art / early build
   screenshots (not real player footage) gives the channel something to publish *now* instead of
   staying dark until every dependency clears, and gives Austin's first CTA something to point new
   followers toward ("subscribe to see who claims these Houses first").
2. **Format precedent.** Kingdom/faction-SMP channels commonly run a "meet the factions" reveal
   before the real launch to seed audience allegiance before day one (viewers pick a House to root
   for before they've even seen it played) — this is a proven pre-launch hype pattern in the genre,
   distinct from Episode 0's job (explain the rules) or Episode 1's job (recap what actually
   happened). Short-form (under 60-90 sec) content is also the primary top-of-funnel discovery lever
   for a zero-subscriber channel per `growth-roadmap-90day.md`'s Week 2-3 plan — this teaser is built
   to cut down into a Short as well as stand alone as a short upload.

**Sits in the schedule as "Episode -1"**: releases before Episode 0, not after — see the updated
row in `content-calendar/launch-arc-schedule.md`.

## Hook (0:00-0:10)

Cold open, no logo. Slow push-in on a cracked, empty stone throne at dusk, five unlit banner poles
standing behind it, no banners raised yet.

**VO:** "Five Houses are about to be founded. None of them exist yet. One of you could be the
first."

Hard cut to title card: **HOUSES OF THE THRONE**.

## The reveal (0:10-1:45) — one beat per House, ~15-18 seconds each

Fast-cut concept-art/build-screenshot stills (or in-progress construction footage if the world is
far enough along by filming time) — one sigil, one location, one line of identity per House, paced
like a trailer, not a documentary:

1. **House Ashwood** — sigil over black. VO: "Ashwood starts on nothing and means to build
   everything." (burnt-tree sigil, poorest starting land)
2. **House Draven** — sigil over grey. VO: "Draven doesn't win wars. Draven decides who wins them."
   (crow sigil, diplomacy/trade-focused)
3. **House Marrow** — sigil over green. VO: "Marrow holds the best land on the map — and everyone
   already resents them for it." (white-stag sigil, "old money" starting position)
4. **House Thorne** — sigil over red. VO: "Thorne isn't waiting for a reason to go to war. Thorne
   is the reason." (bramble sigil, early-aggression House)
5. **House Vell** — sigil over blue. VO: "Vell controls the river. Everyone else just uses it."
   (wave sigil, coastal/trade-route House)

## The stakes (1:45-2:15)

Quick cut back to the empty throne, banners now shown (still unlit/grey) waiting to be claimed.

**VO:** "Five Houses. One Throne. Whoever holds it at the end of the season holds it for real — and
nobody's holding it yet. This isn't a script. Whoever shows up first writes it."

## Call to action (2:15-end)

**VO:** "Houses of the Throne starts soon. Subscribe now to see which House claims the map first —
and if you want to be one of the names in it instead of just watching, that's still on the table."

End card: subscribe prompt, "Episode 0 coming [date]" text overlay (fill in once Episode 0's actual
publish date is set), no server IP yet (server isn't live — don't publish a CTA to join something
that doesn't exist yet; that's Episode 0's job once launch is real).

Composition: the five sigils arranged around a dark, empty throne silhouette, each sigil lit in its
House's color, throne itself unlit/grey in the center. Text overlay, 3-4 words max ("5 HOUSES. 1

the first real test of whether the five sigils read clearly at a glance; if any is hard to
distinguish from another at thumbnail size, flag it back to naming/sigil design before finalizing.
