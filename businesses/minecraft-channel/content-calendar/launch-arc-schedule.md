## Why this cadence

- **Weekly-ish, not daily**: a 1-3 person operation with no editor yet can't sustain daily uploads
  without burning out before Episode 1 even ships — a ~7-day cadence is sustainable and matches
  how Empires-SMP-style channels actually release recap episodes.
- **Episode 3's date is soft on purpose**: unlike 0-2, which can be filmed from server setup and
  early player activity, Episode 3 depends on a real war window actually happening — if server
  population is still near zero by day 17, either delay the episode or reframe it as "why nobody's
  gone to war yet" rather than force a war that doesn't exist.
- **Episode 4 is a safety net**: recap/clip-show format episodes are cheap to produce (mostly
  re-cut existing footage + new VO) and give the series a soft landing point if 0-3 underperform,
  without needing new server drama on demand.
