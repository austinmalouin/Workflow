## What to watch for

**After 2 weeks, answer these:**

1. **Which platform actually pays best per hour?** Not per task — per hour, including hunting time.
   Drop or deprioritize the worst one.
2. **How much of your time is finding work vs. doing work?** If you're spending 30%+ hunting for
   tasks, that's the real problem to solve, and the fix is being on more platforms, not grinding
   harder on one.
3. **Are there better-paying task types?** Some queues pay more than others on the same platform.
   Note which ones in the "Task type" column so you can seek them out.
4. **Is the total realistic against what you need?** If two weeks of honest effort projects to well
   under what rent requires, that's important information *early* — and the steady remote CS job
   goes back on the table as a floor. Better to know at week 2 than week 8.

## Taxes — don't skip this

This is **1099 contractor income.** Nothing is withheld. Nobody is taking taxes out for you.

- **Set aside 25–30% of every payout** in a separate account the moment it lands. Not "later."
- Keep this log — it's your income record if you need to substantiate anything.
- Track any genuine work expenses (portion of internet, a monitor, etc.) — they may be deductible.
- Once income is real, one conversation with a CPA is worth it. Same conversation can cover the
  sole-prop vs. LLC question sitting in the disc golf notes, so it's one appointment for both.
