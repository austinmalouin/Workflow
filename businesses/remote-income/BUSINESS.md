## What this is and why it exists

This is not a venture. It's **Austin's actual income**, and it was created the day he asked a direct
question and got a direct answer:

> "Am I gonna make money off Etsy yes or no?"
> **No.** Not at rent-paying speed, and probably not this year.

The evidence for that answer: **0 visits from Etsy search in 30 days** — not low, zero, meaning the
listings don't rank at all — in one of the most saturated categories on the platform. RIDGEWOOD was
worse (14 sessions/month on generic dropship gear). Every venture in this workspace was at $0 after
months of work. They are asset plays. Rent is not an asset play.

Austin's constraint, stated plainly: he is done with restaurant and hands-on work, it isn't
sustainable for him, and he wants **strictly remote**. This folder exists to get him there.

### Why annotation fits him specifically

Worth writing down because it's not obvious and it drove the recommendation: Austin's own saved
Indeed preferences already listed **Data Entry Clerk, AI Trainer, Search Engine Evaluator, Content
Moderator, Freelance Captioner** — and pointedly *not* call center rep. That instinct is correct.
Phone-based CS is scheduled shifts, live pressure, and constant metric monitoring — for someone who
just said hands-on service work is unsustainable, that's the restaurant with a headset. Async
written work lets him work when he can focus, which is the actual accommodation he needs.

The work itself — judging whether written responses are clear, accurate, and follow instructions —
plays to something he's genuinely good at, and needs no credential.

## Open risk, stated once

Both chosen tracks are **variable income**. Annotation task volume fluctuates; consulting needs
sales before it pays. If the Figure Eight Island Yacht Club paycheck is still coming in, that's the
floor and this plan is fine. **If it isn't, there is no floor under this plan** — and the remote
CS/chat job path is worth reopening as a backstop. Austin did not confirm his runway either way when
asked. Not a nag, just a recorded open question.

## Reality check on the numbers

Deliberately conservative, because the advertised figures in this space are misleading:

- Advertised: "$50–100/hr" on DataAnnotation's Indeed listing.
- **Plan for: $15–20/hr effective.** The high end is specialist work (PhD STEM, niche languages).
- **2–4 weeks to first dollar**, not "start earning today." Approval 1–14 days → unpaid
  qualification tests → paid work → ~1 week to payout.
- Realistic steady state across 2–3 platforms: roughly **$1,000–2,000/month**. Good weeks higher,
  dry weeks lower.
- This is **1099 income** — nothing withheld. Set aside 25–30% for taxes.

## The DataAnnotation constraint (do not lose this)

DataAnnotation pays best and is the hardest to get into: **10–20% acceptance, ONE attempt, no
retakes ever, and using AI to answer the assessment is an instant permanent fail.** It is therefore
deliberately sequenced *fourth*, after Outlier / Alignerr / Mercor, so Austin learns what the work
feels like before spending his single shot.
