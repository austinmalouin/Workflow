## 1. Reset Routine Cards (3 cards)

**Morning Reset**
- Open blinds / let in light
- Make the bed — or just straighten the pillow, that counts
- Drink a glass of water
- 2-minute surface clear (pick one flat space, just one)
- Get dressed — leggings + yesterday's shirt still counts
- *Optional, skip if brain says no*: put a playlist on

**Evening Reset**
- Dishes into the sink or dishwasher — soak, don't scrub-perfect
- Start one load of laundry (it doesn't need to finish tonight)
- Tomorrow's essentials by the door: keys, bag, meds
- Phone on the charger, out of bed's reach
- Lights down, one deep breath

**"Everything Fell Apart" Reset** (the rock-bottom-day card)
- Pick ONE surface. Just one.
- Trash bag sweep — anything obviously trash, gone
- Dishes: rinse and stack, don't wash
- Open a window for 2 minutes
- That's enough today. Truly.

## 2. Room-by-Room Task Breakdown Sheets

Deliberately capped at 5–6 items per room — this is the "no overwhelming full-house lists"
promise from the description. Each room is its own bounded, non-overwhelming card.

**Kitchen Reset**
- Clear counters (dishes to sink)
- Wipe one counter section
- Take out trash/recycling
- Wipe stovetop if used
- Sweep visible crumbs
- Fridge quick scan — toss anything obviously expired

**Bedroom Reset**
- Clothes: clean pile vs. dirty pile (two piles, no more)
- Make bed or straighten blanket
- Clear nightstand surface
- Cups/dishes out
- Trash sweep

**Bathroom Reset**
- Wipe sink & counter
- Toilet quick wipe
- Towels hung or in hamper
- Trash out
- Restock TP/soap if low

**Living Room Reset**
- Pillows/blankets arranged
- Surfaces cleared — remote, cups, etc. back to their spot
- Trash sweep
- Quick tidy of the floor path
- Reset one "catch-all" pile

**Entryway / Catch-All Reset**
- Shoes to their spot
- Mail/packages sorted: keep, toss, or action
- Bags/coats hung
- One "mystery pile" — just 5 minutes on it

## 3. Dopamine-Friendly Check-Off Boxes (design spec, not new copy)

This is a visual/interaction element that runs through every card and sheet above, not a separate
page. **Revised spec** (corrected to match the shop's real style — see review note above; the
ADHD Debt Payoff Kit's live mockup uses plain black-outline square checkboxes, not rounded or
oversized ones):
- Keep the shop's existing plain square checkbox style exactly as-is on the sibling listings — for
  visual consistency across the shop, not a new checkbox design.
- Deliver the "dopamine-friendly" promise through copy, not a new shape: every 3rd item on a sheet
  gets a small italic side-note reward prompt in the same muted-gold accent color already used for
  divider lines — "stretch," "sip water," "30-second dance break," "text a friend a meme" — rotated
  across sheets so it doesn't repeat.
- Keep the reward prompts optional/skippable in tone — never framed as required.

## 4. Brain Dump Page

**Header**: "Get it out of your head"

Three sections, physically separated on the page (this separation is the actual design point —
keeping "tasks" and "worries" apart so worries don't quietly turn into guilt-inducing to-dos):
1. **Tasks buzzing around** — open lined/blank space to just list things
2. **Worries that aren't tasks** — separate open space, explicitly not a to-do list
3. **One thing I can let go of today** — single line

**Footer line**: "You don't have to solve it right now. Just get it out."
