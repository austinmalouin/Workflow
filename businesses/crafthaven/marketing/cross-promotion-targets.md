# Cross-Promotion Targets

Researched 2026-08-10 (WebSearch). Real communities found, not a generic list — but **none of these
have been posted to yet**; CraftHaven has no server, no Discord, and nothing to promote. This is a
target list to use once there's something real to point people at, per the
`crafthaven-growth` skill's instruction to find and respect each community's specific self-promotion
rules rather than assume blanket posting is fine.

## Server-promotion-friendly subreddits (advertising is the point)

These exist specifically for Minecraft server owners to advertise — lowest-friction targets once
CraftHaven actually launches:

- **r/MCServers** — dedicated server-promotion subreddit. Rule found: owners can post once per
  week, must tag the post properly, and the server must run in online-mode (cracked/offline-mode
  servers get removed) — confirm CraftHaven's Apex Hosting config is online-mode before posting.
- **r/MinecraftServer** — similar server-promotion subreddit; looser cadence found in research
  (roughly once per 24 hours allowed), but **verify current rules directly on the subreddit before
  posting** — this was general research, not a direct read of the current sidebar.
- **r/Admincraft** — broader server-admin community (not purely promo-focused); likely useful for
  "how did you structure your Houses/economy plugins" style discussion posts that double as organic
  awareness, more than direct ads. Check its specific self-promo rules before posting an ad there.

## ASOIAF/GoT fan communities (discussion-first, NOT promo-friendly by default)

These are where the target audience already congregates, but they are book/show discussion spaces,
not server-promo spaces — treat direct advertising here as a rules violation until proven otherwise:

- **r/ASOIAF** (1.5M+ subscribers) — large, active, book/show-focused discussion community. No
  self-promotion or off-topic policy was confirmed in this session's research (search returned
  general Reddit self-promo guidance, not r/ASOIAF's actual sidebar rules). **Do not post there
  without first reading the current rules directly** — if there's no Minecraft/gaming-crossover
  allowance, the realistic play is organic participation (being a genuine community member) rather
  than any promotional post.
- **"The Free Cities"** Discord — 18+ community for ASOIAF/GoT/House of the Dragon discussion,
  edits, memes, art. Community hub, not a promo board — same caution as above; look for a
  self-promo channel/thread before posting anything, and ask a mod first if none is visible.
- **"Dunk'n Scones"** Discord — LGBTQ+-friendly ASOIAF discussion server with organized
  media-specific channels. Same caution.
- Several **ASOIAF-themed Minecraft-adjacent roleplay Discords** turned up in research (new servers
  opened Jan 2026, Jun 2026, and Jul 31 2026) — these are the closest existing analogues (GoT-themed
  + Discord/game crossover audience already self-selected). Worth a direct look once CraftHaven is
  live: these are potential collaboration/cross-promotion partners (per the `growth-roadmap-90day.md`
  contingency plan's "same-tier creators, not big names" pattern) rather than places to cold-post an
  ad, since a same-size, complementary community swap is a two-way trade both sides would want.
