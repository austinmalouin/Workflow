# Naming Proposal — Server, Throne Mechanic, and Starter Houses

Drafted 2026-07-23, to unblock the "pick final (non-trademarked) naming" step flagged as the
server's next concrete milestone in `BUSINESS.md`. These are original names built to evoke the
GoT-adjacent feel without using any HBO-owned terms (no "Thrones," "Stark," "Lannister," "Westeros,"
etc.) — see the naming/IP note in `BUSINESS.md` for why that matters once real money is involved.
This is a proposal to choose from or riff on, not a final decision — that stays Austin's call.

## Server name — DECIDED 2026-07-23: CraftHaven

Austin picked **CraftHaven** over this doc's three original proposals. Checked for name conflicts
via WebSearch before locking it in: the name has been reused by a few small/inactive servers over
the years (a 2016-17 PvP/factions listing, a crafthaven.net SMP listing currently 0 players online
and "offline") but nothing with real brand recognition or a live player base — judged clear.

The three originals below are kept for reference / repurposing (e.g. as a season or Throne-mechanic
name) but are no longer live candidates for the server name itself:

1. **Sundered Realm** — evokes a kingdom broken into competing Houses; short, easy to say in a
   Discord invite or YouTube title.
2. **The Hollow Crown** — literary-sounding (borrows the phrase's public-domain Shakespeare
   association, not a trademarked one), fits an "empty throne" launch narrative directly.
3. **Ashfall Reach** — more original-fantasy-coded, less directly GoT-coded if Austin wants more
   distance from the comparison while keeping the tone.

## The Throne mechanic's in-game name

Avoid "Iron Throne" directly. Options: **the Seat of Ash**, **the Broken Seat**, **the First Seat**.
Recommendation: **the Broken Seat** — implies contested/unstable, which matches the seasonal
control mechanic (nobody holds it permanently) better than "First" or "Ash."

## Starter House names (originals, not reskins of specific GoT houses)

A new server needs a few pre-built "founding" Houses ready before real players claim their own, so
Episode 0/1 footage has something to show. Five originals, each with a one-line identity hook a
new player can grab onto immediately:

1. **House Ashwood** — sigil: a burnt tree over black. Identity: "we rebuild on ruin" — good fit
   for a House that starts on contested/poor land and rises through play, not privilege.
2. **House Draven** — sigil: a crow over grey. Identity: information/alliances — a diplomatic
   House that thrives on trade deals and intelligence rather than raw territory.
3. **House Marrow** — sigil: a white stag over green. Identity: the "old money" House — holds the
   best starting land, gives new players something to resent/rally against narratively.
4. **House Thorne** *(no relation to "the Throne" — deliberately close-but-distinct, a real-world
   naming trick used in fiction to create instant association without infringing)* — sigil: a
   bramble over red. Identity: the aggressive early-war House.
5. **House Vell** — sigil: a wave over blue. Identity: coastal/trade House, controls river/port
   access — a natural rival to House Marrow's inland holdings.

## Why five, and why these five

Five starter Houses gives the launch video (Episode 0) and the founding episodes (1-2) enough
built-in rivalry pairs (Ashwood vs. Marrow as underdog-vs-old-money, Thorne vs. everyone as the
early-war catalyst, Draven as the swing/diplomatic vote, Vell as the geographic wildcard) without
overcrowding a map that also needs room for real players to found their own Houses. This mirrors
how Empires-SMP-style series front-load 4-6 distinct starting factions so episode 1 already has
texture instead of an empty map with no story yet.
