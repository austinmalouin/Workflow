## Discussion prompts (Discord — post once Discord exists)

1. **"The finale just aired — who made the smartest move this season?"** Open-ended HotD S3
   discussion starter, no spoiler-tagging needed once posted after the finale's air date. Purpose:
   draw in ASOIAF-adjacent members with a topic they're already thinking about, then pivot the
   thread naturally toward "which CraftHaven House philosophy does that character remind you of"
   once Houses exist — plants the House-identity hook without forcing it.
2. **"If you were founding a House right now, what's the first thing you'd secure — land, allies,
   or a story?"** Primes early joiners to think in House-strategy terms before the server is even
   live; doubles as market research (which starting resource people say they'd prioritize is a real
   signal for balancing the five starter Houses).
3. **"Sworn-Houses trivia — Wednesday."** Light, low-effort recurring slot: a general Seven-Kingdoms-
   style political trivia question (succession, alliances, betrayals — genre trivia, not
   server-specific yet since Houses aren't public). Cheap to produce, keeps a channel active between
   real announcements.
4. **"Name the trope you want CraftHaven to nail, and the one you want it to avoid."** Direct
   feedback-gathering framed as a discussion, not a survey — real input Austin can use before
   locking remaining design decisions (Throne-mechanic name, House rosters).
